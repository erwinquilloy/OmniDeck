OmniDeck 0.4.test for FrSky Ethos radios (X20 family 800x480, X18 480x320)
One telemetry display for ArduPilot, INAV and Betaflight.
Avionics + moving map telemetry widget for ArduPilot, INAV and Betaflight.





WHAT'S NEW IN 0.4.test (test build, after 0.4.0)
- PARAMS page over ELRS and TBS Crossfire (150 Hz), not only S.Port / F.Port. Needs ArduPlane
  Stratos firmware with "MAVlite over CRSF" (v2.2.1 or the test builds). Upstream ArduPilot and
  ELRS MAVLink mode get the page "The flight controller doesn't answer". TBS at 50 Hz is too
  slow for it for now: use 150 Hz.
- mLRS: the PARAMS page, the ArduPilot mission download and the mission editor's Send work over
  mLRS with ANY ArduPilot (no Stratos firmware needed). In the mLRS Lua (Tx section) set
  "Tx Ser Port" = cbridge, then store / reboot the module. The mLRS module then passes MAVLink
  between the radio and the aircraft and OmniDeck talks to ArduPilot directly; telemetry on the
  screens stays as before. ("Tx Ser Port2" offers no cbridge in mLRS 1.4; it can stay on wbridge
  for Mission Planner over WiFi.) The bridge was checked on a RadioMaster Nomad with mLRS 1.4.04.
  Over mLRS the editor's Send uses ArduPilot's normal upload (as Mission Planner); OmniDeck still
  refuses it while armed.
- ArduPilot mission download (setting "ArduPilot mission download", Off by default): the mission
  on the aircraft is shown on the map, takeoff and land marked (T / L). Needs the Stratos mission
  test firmware (or mLRS with cbridge, see above). Checked every 10 s while disarmed and downloaded again when it changed (a new
  upload from Mission Planner). The Sensor check page shows "mission: N items". Read only.
- Switching the setting Off takes the downloaded mission off the map again.
- Mission editor (X20 family only; the X18's smaller screen shows the downloaded mission but has no editor): on the map page, EDIT (shown with "ArduPilot mission download" On and
  an ArduPilot flight controller). Make or change the mission on the radio and send it to the
  aircraft. Needs the Stratos mission test firmware, or mLRS with cbridge.
  - It starts from the downloaded mission, or a new one (a takeoff at home).
  - Tap the map: a waypoint after the selected item. Tap a point: select it. Tap the left / right
    third of the info line (top): the previous / next item, also those not on the map (speed
    change, land start, RTL, transition).
  - Type: Waypoint, Loiter (unlimited), Loiter turns, Loiter time, Land, RTL, Speed change, Land start; on a quadplane
    (Q_ENABLE 1) also VTOL land and Transition, and Type on the first item switches Takeoff /
    VTOL takeoff.
  - Field picks what - / + change: altitude, loiter turns, loiter radius, loiter time, speed,
    transition direction (to FW / to VTOL). A timed loiter has no radius: ArduPilot uses
    WP_LOITER_RAD for it.
  - < / > move the item earlier / later (the takeoff stays first). Del deletes it and selects the
    item before it, so a tap on the map puts a new point in its place (to move a waypoint: Del,
    then tap where it should be). Del twice on the takeoff starts a new mission. The wheel changes
    the field, ENTER selects the next item.
  - Send (disarmed only): the mission is cleared, written item by item and read back: "Sent and
    verified". "Refused: aircraft armed" / "Refused: ..." show ArduPilot's reason.
  - A downloaded mission with commands the editor can't keep (servo, camera, ...) is not opened:
    it would lose them. Del twice on the takeoff for a new one, or edit it in a GCS.
  - Done leaves the editor (an unsent mission is dropped).

WHAT'S NEW IN 0.4.0
- PARAMS page (ArduPilot only, S.Port / F.Port): read and change ArduPlane parameters from the
  radio, over ArduPilot's own FrSky passthrough uplink (no firmware change). Switch it on with
  "ArduPilot params page" (Off by default); it then joins the page cycle when the flight controller is
  ArduPilot. Over Crossfire, ELRS and mLRS the page says it needs S.Port or F.Port.
  - Tabs: FLIGHT, LAUNCH, RETURN, FAILSAFE, ARMING, TECS, TRIM, PID, VTOL, Q PID (VTOL and
    Q PID only with Q_ENABLE 1). Values are read one by one while the page is open.
  - Wheel: pick a row. ENTER: open the editor (with no row picked, ENTER turns the page as
    always). In the editor the wheel changes the value, ENTER saves, RTN goes back. Touch: tap a
    tab or a row; the editor has - + Back Save buttons.
  - ARMING_SKIPCHK opens as a list of checks, each CHECK or SKIP.
  - A save is sent once and shows "Saved and verified" only when the value read back from the
    flight controller matches. It is never repeated by itself; "No answer" means check the value
    in a GCS.
  - Armed: view only. Setting "ArduPilot params: edit in flight" = "Gains and trim" lets the PID, Q PID and
    TRIM rows be changed in flight (one wheel click at a time, with height to spare).
  - A parameter the flight controller doesn't have shows "n/a".
- params.txt: your own rows, added to the built-in list (one file for all models). See
  scripts/omnideck/params_example.txt for the rules; rename it to params.txt to use it.
- Sensor check page: a "Params:" line (MAVlite frames sent / received / bad, params.txt rows).

WHAT'S NEW IN 0.3.0 (after 0.3b; X20 family and X18-size screens)
- ArduPilot over TBS Crossfire at 50 Hz (forced, or Dynamic dropping to 50 Hz at range): ArduPilot
  then sends its telemetry in one burst every 3 seconds, and the screen stepped every 3 s with
  glitches between bursts. OmniDeck now reads ArduPilot's own "CRSF: Link rate 50Hz" message and,
  only on a TBS link below 150 Hz:
  - no red NO TELEMETRY flash between bursts, speeds no longer blank out, READY TO ARM works;
  - ground speed, satellites and distance to home update every second, and voltage, current and
    mAh every 2 s, from the standard Crossfire GPS and battery sensors between bursts.
  Horizon, airspeed, altitude and flight mode still update every 3 s: only ArduPilot's burst
  carries them. ELRS, mLRS, F.Port, S.Port, INAV, Betaflight and TBS at 150 Hz: unchanged.
  It switches on at the first 3-second pause after ArduPilot's message, so that first pause may
  still flash once.
  The Crossfire GPS and battery sensors must be discovered (Model > Telemetry > Discover new
  sensors) for the faster values. A real link loss on that 50 Hz link shows after up to 7 s if
  Ethos does not report it sooner (it usually does, with its own telemetry-lost flag).
- Satellites above 15 with ArduPilot: its telemetry has room for 15 at most, so 18 showed as 15.
  Over Crossfire / ELRS the real count now comes from the standard GPS sensor (discover sensors
  once); over S.Port / F.Port, where 15 is all ArduPilot sends, the top bar shows "15+".
- Map page + and - buttons: a tap near a button, in the gap between them, or one that rolls off the
  edge as you lift your finger now zooms instead of turning the page. A tap elsewhere on the map
  still turns the page.
- Sensor check page: when the rows don't all fit, they scroll under the header lines (wheel, or
  drag up and down; a scrollbar shows at the right edge). A tap still turns the page.

WHAT'S NEW IN 0.3b - public beta (test 43; X20 family and X18-size screens)
- HUD home marker (the H under the heading box): it now uses the whole width of the heading strip
  for +-90 degrees, so it shows up as soon as home is anywhere ahead, not only within 45 degrees
  of the nose. With home further round than 90 degrees it stays parked at the end of the strip on
  home's side (turn that way); it goes only with home right behind (within 5 degrees of the tail).
  Its place no longer matches the compass letters above it (those still span 90 degrees).
- Map page: the PLANE / HOME button is gone; + and - are bigger square buttons, easier to hit.
  The map view (aircraft or home in the middle) is still the "Map view" setting.
- Home on the map and minimap: the home icon now sits in a black box with a blue frame. Off the
  map, the box on the edge also holds the distance to home (on the minimap too).
- Fresh start on a model switch and at power-on: the telemetry, the trail, the flight time, the
  last flight's summary, the mission, the alarms and the last GPS position are cleared, the
  summary page closes, and the welcome is said again. The last position (shown as LAST on the map
  when the fix is lost) is kept only while the same model stays selected and the radio stays on;
  lastpos.txt is no longer used (you can delete it from /scripts/omnideck/).
- Map tiles load much faster (the way the ETHOS Mapping Widget does it): a whole screen is
  queued in a few updates instead of 2 tiles per update, nearest the aircraft first. Tile sets in
  PNG, and Yaapu's Google folders, now show too (Ethos gives no error for a missing file, so the
  first format tried was taken for every tile).
- Flight stats page: with "Flight stats page" On it is always in the page cycle, also in flight,
  where it shows the flight in progress (FLIGHT TIME (NOW), distance and maximums so far); before
  the first flight it says "No flight yet". Off: never in the cycle. Either way the flight
  summary still opens on disarm ("Flight summary on disarm") and now goes back to the main page
  by itself after 15 s; tap or turn to another page first to leave it sooner. A stats page you
  open yourself stays.
- THR with INAV and Betaflight: they send no throttle in their telemetry, so the THR block now
  shows your radio's throttle stick (0-100 %), as iNav LuaTelemetry does. It is your command, not
  the motor: in INAV's automatic modes (RTH, cruise, auto launch, failsafe) the motor can differ.
  With ArduPilot THR is still the real throttle output sent by the flight controller.
- Flight logs: each flight that opens the flight summary (10 s or more armed) is saved as its own
  file in /scripts/omnideck/logs/, named like the radio's telemetry logs:
  <model>-<YYYY-MM-DD>-<HHMMSS>.txt (e.g. "Talon Pro-2026-11-10-144336.txt"). It holds the date
  and time, model, FC and link, the summary's figures, home, the last position and the last 10
  messages. Written once, at the disarm. Copy the whole package so the logs folder is there.
- Betaflight airmode: AIR is shown and spoken "Air mode", ACRO stays ACRO (the test 42 fix; a copy
  of test 42 sent before it was added still showed ACRO for both).

WHAT'S NEW IN TEST 42 (X20 family and X18-size screens)
Main page
- Data blocks: HOME (with TOT, the distance flown, as its note) | FLIGHT TIME, then ALT | AS / GS
  (current values; the maximums are on the flight summary), Battery | USED, CURR | THR. With no
  airspeed (no pitot over mLRS, Betaflight) the AS / GS block shows GS alone.
- The HOME block's small arrow is gone. Home direction is now:
  - on the HUD: a white H in a black box under the heading box, at home's bearing on the compass
    strip's scale, shown while home is within 45 degrees of the nose (hidden further round);
  - in the top-right panel, with "Home arrow" chosen (below).
- HUD tapes: labelled with the unit alone (km/h, m), one font size bigger than before.
- With no airspeed, the speed tape's value box is green (as the GS box), so it is clear the tape
  shows ground speed.
- Vario bar: no dark background any more; the climb / sink bar and its zero line sit on the sky
  and ground.
- Top-right panel, setting "Main page top-right panel": Minimap, Home arrow, Radar, OmniDeck logo,
  Logo file. Home arrow: the OmniDeck logo before arming, then the logo's own arrow pointing home
  (up = home straight ahead). Logo file: the PNG picked in "Logo file" (Stratos, or your own in
  /scripts/omnideck/logos/; filled to the panel, smaller ones not enlarged).
  X18-size screens can show the minimap again (test 41's Lua savings leave room). A model set up
  before keeps what it showed.
Pages (tap or ENTER)
- ArduPilot: Main, Map, Messages. INAV / Betaflight: Main, Map (they send no messages).
  Setting "Messages page": Auto (ArduPilot only), On, Off.
- "Flight stats page" (Off by default): On puts the last flight's summary in the cycle. The
  summary still opens by itself on disarm ("Flight summary on disarm").
- "Sensor check page" (Off by default): On puts the sensor check page in the cycle.
- "ArduPilot params page" (Off by default): On puts the PARAMS page in the cycle (ArduPilot only).
  "ArduPilot params: edit in flight": Off, or "Gains and trim" (PID, Q PID and TRIM rows while armed).
- "ArduPilot mission download" (Off by default): the aircraft's mission on the map (Stratos firmware).
Fixes
- Betaflight with airmode on (permanently or by switch): the mode shows AIR and is spoken "Air
  mode"; with airmode off it shows ACRO. It used to show ACRO for both. (Over S.Port Betaflight
  sends no airmode flag, and current INAV always sends ACRO.)
- ArduPilot over ELRS or TBS Crossfire: the map now follows the aircraft. ArduPilot sends its
  position over CRSF only as the standard GPS sensor, not in passthrough, so the map stayed on the
  last saved position. mLRS, R9 / F.Port and INAV / Betaflight were not affected.
- Betaflight 4.5 over CRSF: altitude was about the field's height above sea level too low (MAX ALT
  -278 m). Betaflight sends the GPS's sea-level altitude while disarmed and the height above the
  arming point once armed; the altitude taken at arming was the sea-level one.
- Map trail: a smooth line of the last 20 seconds (a point every 0.25 s, every point drawn); it
  was up to 300 points drawn every 3rd or so, so circles showed corners. About 20 % less Lua
  work on the map page than before.
- X18-size screens: a logo fills the whole panel (208 x 123 px; best size 204 x 119, a square one
  about 119 x 119; never enlarged). It was held to 107 x 107.
- X18-size screens, from the X20's test 41b:
  - FrSky links (S.Port / F.Port / FBUS, R9): LQ is read from the receiver's VFR sensor (the % of
    frames received), and RSSI is shown on the 0-100 scale the flight controller's own OSD uses
    (INAV / Betaflight as sent, ArduPilot's 0-50 doubled).
  - The welcome is said again after switching to another model.
- Changed since test 41 / 41b: x20/ and x18/ hud.lua, main.lua, map.lua, telem.lua, ui.lua.

WHAT'S NEW IN TEST 37
- ArduPilot over S.Port / F.Port / FBUS on radios whose Lua uses 32-bit numbers (the X18;
  page 5 shows "32-bit LOST"): every value now arrives exact. The flight mode read -1, status

PUBLIC BETA. OmniDeck is a display: keep the radio's own telemetry alarms and the flight
controller's failsafes as your safety net. Do not rely on it, or on its voice call-outs, alone.

  OmniDeck_Ethos_Suite_0.4.test.zip   install with Ethos Suite (A)
  OmniDeck_Ethos_0.4.test.zip         copy by hand (B)
Both put OmniDeck in scripts/omnideck/. On radios with built-in storage (X18, X20 Pro/R/RS)
that is the internal drive unless the radio uses an SD card; wherever these steps say "card",
read the drive that holds the scripts folder.

A. With Ethos Suite
1. Connect the radio to the PC by USB and start Ethos Suite.
2. Lua Library > Install from local .zip > pick OmniDeck_Ethos_Suite_0.4.test.zip.
   The Suite installs to scripts/omnideck/ on the radio's storage.
3. If scripts/omnideck/missions/ is not there afterwards, make it for ArduPilot .waypoints
   files. The logs folder appears with the first flight log.
4. Disconnect, restart the radio, and go on at step 4 of B.

B. By hand
1. Connect the radio to the PC by USB and choose the SD card (or use a card reader).
2. Copy the "scripts" folder from this package to the ROOT of the card. You should end up with:
       scripts/omnideck/main.lua
       scripts/omnideck/x18/   scripts/omnideck/x20/   (the OmniDeck code, one copy per screen size)
       scripts/omnideck/audio/   scripts/omnideck/tones/   scripts/omnideck/logos/
       scripts/omnideck/missions/   scripts/omnideck/logs/
   The radio picks x18/ (screens 480 px wide or less) or x20/ by itself; nothing to set.
3. Eject the card and restart the radio.
4. Add OmniDeck to a screen with a FULL-SCREEN layout. In a smaller slot it only shows the minimap.
5. Tap the screen or press ENTER to change page: Main, Map, Messages, and the flight summary
   after a flight. The wheel zooms the map and scrolls the messages.

MAP TILES are not in either package. Put them on the same drive as scripts/omnideck:
       bitmaps/ethosmaps/maps/<PROVIDER>/<Type>/<z>/<x>/<y>.jpg
   Guide: https://erwinquilloy.github.io/ardupilot-tools/omnideck.html#tiles

UPDATING: install the newer Suite zip, or copy the new scripts folder over the old one. Either
way, then delete any .luac files in scripts/omnideck/, x18/ and x20/ (old compiled copies).
   From test 41 or earlier: in scripts/omnideck/ also delete the old .lua and .luac files
   (audio.lua, hud.lua, map.lua, mission.lua, mspclient.lua, summary.lua, telem.lua, tiles.lua,
   ui.lua) but keep main.lua (the new one replaces it) and the folders.
   If you installed the earlier test build called AvMap, delete scripts/avmap.


TELEMETRY SETUP
ArduPilot (FrSky passthrough, the same data Yaapu uses):
  CRSF/ELRS/TBS Crossfire: set RC_OPTIONS bit 8 (CRSF passthrough). With ELRS keep packet rate x
              telemetry ratio above about 30 telemetry frames per second.
  S.Port (R9 etc.): SERIALn_PROTOCOL 10      F.Port: SERIALn_PROTOCOL 23
  mLRS:       set the Tx module to send CRSF passthrough to the radio.
INAV / Betaflight: nothing to set on the aircraft beyond normal telemetry. Discover the sensors
  on the radio (Model > Telemetry > Discover new sensors) before using OmniDeck.
LINK SETTINGS on the transmitter:
  ELRS:          telemetry ratio 1:8 or more often (1:4, 1:2).
  TBS Crossfire: Dynamic or 150 Hz forced. At 50 Hz the telemetry runs seconds late with any
                 flight controller, and Dynamic drops to 50 Hz far out.
  mLRS, F.Port:  the defaults work.


SETTINGS (long-press the widget > Configure widget)
- Voice: Aria or Guy, or Off. Picking one plays a short test clip.
  If you have Yaapu's English sounds, a copy of that folder in scripts/omnideck/audio/ also works.
- Message beep: a short tone on new status messages (every message, warnings and errors, or off).
- Battery mAh (INAV/BF only): your pack size. INAV and Betaflight don't send it; ArduPilot sends
  BATT_CAPACITY. The battery bar needs it, and a working current sensor.
- Efficiency units, speed and altitude units, map orientation, map tiles, flight summary on disarm.


MAP TILES (optional; without them you get a vector map with range rings)
1. On the PC open https://martinovem.github.io/High-Resolution-Map-Generator/ in Chrome or Edge.
2. Output Target: "b14ckyy ETHOS Mapping Widget". Pick provider and type (e.g. Google Satellite)
   and zoom 12 to 17 around your field. Draw a box bigger than where you fly.
3. Save to the ROOT of the SD card (or extract the ZIP there). Check the path, capitals included:
       bitmaps/ethosmaps/maps/GOOGLE/Satellite/15/...
4. Restart the radio and pick the set under "Map tiles" in the widget settings.


WHAT TO REPORT
Please send: radio model and Ethos version, flight controller (ArduPilot/INAV/Betaflight and
version), the link (ELRS, Crossfire, mLRS, R9...), and for any problem a photo or video of the
screen. If a script error shows, the exact text.
Things worth checking:
- Do the flight mode, armed/disarmed and READY TO ARM / NOT READY TO ARM show correctly?
- Does the horizon move the right way when you roll and pitch the aircraft?
- Do airspeed, ground speed (green box), altitude, home distance and the home arrow look right?
- Do you hear the voice call-outs and message beeps, and are they loud enough?
- After a flight of more than 10 s, does the flight summary open on disarm?
- With tiles: do they line up with the aircraft?
