## OmniDeck 0.3.0

One telemetry display for ArduPilot, INAV and Betaflight: horizon, moving map with satellite or street tiles, messages, flight summary and voice call-outs. Includes the Aria and Guy voice packs.

**New in 0.3.0**
- TBS Crossfire at 50 Hz with ArduPilot: no false NO TELEMETRY flashes between bursts; ground speed, satellites, home distance and battery keep updating. Force 150 Hz for a live horizon.
- More than 15 satellites with ArduPilot: the real count over Crossfire and ELRS, "15+" over S.Port and F.Port.
- Map + and - buttons accept taps near them instead of turning the page.

**After installing:** restart the radio and add OmniDeck to a full-screen layout. Map tiles are not in the package: copy them to bitmaps/ethosmaps/maps/ on the same drive as the scripts.

Guide: https://erwinquilloy.github.io/ardupilot-tools/omnideck.html
